# Code of conduct

Be respectful, constructive and considerate. Welcome contributors with different backgrounds and levels of experience. Critique ideas and code, not people.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, sexualized conduct and deliberate disruption are not acceptable in issues, pull requests or other project spaces. Do not publish private information or credentials.

Maintainers may edit or remove content, close discussions, or restrict participation to protect contributors. Apply moderation proportionately and explain decisions where practical. Report concerns privately to the maintainer through their GitHub profile, without posting another person's private details publicly. Maintainers are expected to follow the same standards.
